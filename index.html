About the laboratory

Created in January 2026 by the French National Centre for Scientific Research (CNRS), the University of the State of Rio de Janeiro (UERJ) and the Federal Fluminense University (UFF), the IRL MAREL aims to improve knowledge on all aspects of eutrophication processes in tropical coastal zones.

Biogeochemical cycles

Carbon, nitrogen and phosphorus cycles, spreading eutrophication at the land-sea interface.

Mangrove vulnerability

Response of mangrove ecosystems to eutrophication including geochemical and microbial processes in urban and preserved mangroves.

Biological communities

Phytoplankton dynamics, harmful algal blooms, trophic fluxes and changes in food webs, bio-indicators of eutrophication.
